About Us

Born from enterprise-level strategy and reimagined for scalable real-world deployment, SIT GroundForce™ represents the evolution of Applied Branding & Marketing’s growth philosophy.

After years supporting Fortune-level initiatives and emerging ventures alike, our leadership recognized a major gap in the marketplace: businesses had access to digital marketing — but lacked structured field infrastructure capable of delivering consistent local presence.

Today we help organizations bridge that gap through Strategic Industry Territory outreach, combining professional oversight, performance-driven training environments, and structured field deployment teams designed for both regional and national expansion.

We don’t replace marketing — we strengthen it with real-world engagement.
